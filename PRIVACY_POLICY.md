# Privacy Policy (Draft)

Last updated: 2026-01-04

## Overview
Archie helps you analyze product safety. To provide results, the app processes scan images on our servers and stores scan history on your device. If you sign in, we sync history and scan images to our servers so they are available across devices.

## Information We Collect
- Account details when you sign in (name, email, user ID).
- Scan images you submit and the safety results derived from them.
- Scan history (product name, scores, timestamps).

## How We Use Information
- Run safety analysis and show results.
- Sync history and images across devices when you sign in.
- Maintain app functionality and improve reliability.

## Data Storage and Processing
- Local storage on your device for history and thumbnails.
- Server-side storage for analysis and signed-in sync.
- We use third-party service providers such as Google Sign-In and Firebase (Authentication, Storage).

## Sharing and Disclosure
- We share data with service providers that process it on our behalf.
- We do not sell personal information and do not use your data for advertising or tracking.

## Your Choices
- You can use the app without signing in.
- You can sign out to stop syncing.
- You can remove local data by deleting the app.

## Data Retention
We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service and comply with legal obligations.

## Security
We use reasonable safeguards to protect information.

## Children's Privacy
Archie is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Contact
Contact us using the support email listed in the App Store listing.
